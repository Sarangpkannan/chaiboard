📋 Chaiboard Health Engine Calculation Standards (PRD Section 4)

Calories Profile:

Calculated from whole milk and sugar content of standard campus tapri cutting chai. Excess triggers mental marathon notifications.

Sugar Intake Profile:

Based on ~2 teaspoons of granulated sugar per 100ml. Crossing 30 grams notifies student to switch to light sweet or fika cutting.

Caffeine Load Profile:

Slow boiled Assam / CTC tea leaves release approximately 30mg bioavailable caffeine per glass, pacing late night study sessions.
